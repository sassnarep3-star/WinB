Five events, and one thing this site cannot measure

Click and conversion measurement was asked for before any content was written. Half of that is possible from a static site. The other half lives inside an account this build cannot see. Here is exactly where the line falls.

Why conversions are not on this page

Reported orders exist only inside the Associates reporting interface, behind the owner's sign-in. No API key, session or credential is held by this build, and none should be. Any conversion rate, earnings figure or revenue forecast printed on this site would therefore be invented. S04 defines when a qualifying purchase happens — a click through a Special Link, then a session that can run up to 24 hours, with cart items qualifying up to 89 days — and reading that definition is the closest this site can honestly get to a conversion number.

The campaign ledger starts empty for the same reason: a ledger with invented opening balances teaches you nothing and misleads anyone who reads it later.

What has to be true before these events mean anything

A browser-local counter proves nothing beyond the browser it ran in. For these five events to become a measurement, one of three things must happen: a serverless endpoint accepts the POST, a hosted analytics product is configured, or the counts are read manually from a device. Each option has a different disclosure consequence under S02 section 3(e). That decision is Q-05, and until it is made the honest statement is: measurement is instrumented, not yet collected.

What the site refuses to record

  • No unique visitor identifier, so no individual can be followed across a session.
  • No cross-site identifiers, and no fingerprinting.
  • No sub-tag per visitor: S02 explicitly forbids dynamically assigning sub-tags to users to monitor their behaviour.
  • No stored referrer beyond the referring host.
