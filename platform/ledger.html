System · Ledger

The campaign ledger

A ledger with a defined shape and zero rows. It will be filled from authorised first-party reports, one dated entry at a time, or it will stay empty. There is no placeholder number in it, no estimate, and no forecast.

DateSourceChannelEligible sessionsClicksReported ordersProvenance
——————No authorised report obtained

Column definitions, so an entry means something

ColumnDefinitionWhere the value must come from
DateThe date the report was generated, not the date of the traffic.The report header.
SourceThe referring channel or property.The report, not an attribution guess.
ChannelThe page or campaign that produced the traffic.The analytics endpoint, once configured.
Eligible sessionsSessions that could have produced a qualifying purchase — the denominator.Associates report or the analytics endpoint.
ClicksOutbound clicks attributed to the tag.The Associates report. The client-side counter cannot substitute: it counts departures, not attributions.
Reported ordersOrders as reported. Not modelled, not estimated, not annualised.The Associates report.
ProvenanceA link or file reference to the exact report this row came from.Wherever the report is archived.

Why it is empty rather than seeded

A demonstration ledger with plausible-looking numbers would be the single most dangerous artefact this project could produce: it would look like evidence, be cited as evidence, and be impossible to distinguish later from a real entry. An empty table can only be read one way.
