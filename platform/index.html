System

What the machinery actually does

The brief asked for an automated affiliate platform. This page is an honest inventory of what was automated, what was deliberately left to a human, and why the second list is longer than the first.

Capability asked forWhat exists nowWhat it doesWhat it cannot do
Product researchThe product register (data/products.json)Records five ASINs with brand, category path, listing title and an observation date, plus a labelled strengths/trade-offs pair per product.Discover new products. There is no crawler, because the licence forbids one.
Identify trending, high-converting productsNot built — deliberatelyA "trending" score would need sales or search data the site does not have. Publishing one would be invention.Anything. It was refused rather than faked.
Analyse pricingNot built — blockedPrice may be displayed only from an Amazon-served source or the API. No such source is connected.Showing a price at all. See Q-13.
Analyse reviews and competitionNot builtExtracting review text is prohibited by the IP licence, and comparing competitors would need a second data source.Both. Listed so the gap is visible.
Commission potentialNot builtNo commission rate was verified for these products, so no rate, estimate or forecast appears anywhere.Ranking products by payout. The site ranks by cooking decision instead.
SEO-optimised articles and comparisonsBuiltFour guides, three comparison tables, a sitemap, a robots file and an RSS feed, generated at build time.Knowing whether any of it ranks. That needs measurement, not markup.
Publish and manage affiliate linksBuilt, with a gateOne link helper generates every buy URL as a sponsored interstitial. A checker fails the build on a bad link.Guaranteeing the links track. That is Q-02.
Track clicks and conversionsClicks: built client-side. Conversions: not possibleFive defined events, no cookie, no identifier. Conversions live only in Associates reports.Closing the loop without the owner.
Continuously optimise campaignsPlanned, gatedThe growth plan sets a cadence with pre-registered thresholds so "optimisation" is a decision rule rather than a feeling.Running unattended. Nothing changes live without a human reading the data.
Compliance with Associates policyBuilt, and the strongest partEleven specific clauses are mapped to the design change each one caused, on the compliance page.Legal advice, or confirming the account exists.

The honest headline

Roughly a third of the brief was automatable without violating the Associates licence, and that third is done. The rest needs either an API approval the owner must request, or a human looking at a registry. Calling that "AI-powered optimisation" would be marketing language applied to a file of five products, and this project's whole editorial position is that labelled evidence beats confident language.
