What to check before you decide

Our reasoning from the listing — labelled as inference, because that is what it is.

  • The ±0.5°F figure and the NIST/NSF certification are the seller's claims in the listing; the certifying bodies' own registries were not checked. Treat as a claim to verify, not a tested fact. Editorial inference
  • This is an instant-read probe, not a leave-in wireless probe. It does not give you a reading from across the kitchen. Editorial inference
  • 1 second is a speed claim. Speed and accuracy can trade off in cheap sensors, so the only real test is a calibrated ice bath and boiling water. Editorial inference
