Privacy notice

What this site stores, which is close to nothing, and what happens after you click a product link.

Updated 2026-11-20Applies to every page on this site

The short version

This site sets no cookies. It runs no third-party scripts, no advertising pixels, and no social embeds. It does not build a profile of you and it does not try to identify you.

Measurement

The site includes a small first-party script that counts five events: a page view, a click on an Amazon link, a click on a product card, completion of a guide, and a subscription. Each event carries the event name, the page path, and for product clicks the product identifier. Nothing else.

  • No cookies and no local storage.
  • No identifiers, hashes, or fingerprints of any kind.
  • No cross-site tracking, and no sharing with any advertiser network.
  • If your browser sends Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control, the script sends nothing at all.

As of this notice, the collector endpoint is not configured, so no measurement data is being recorded. The measurement page states the same thing, and it will be updated on the same day the endpoint goes live.

When you click a product link

Product links open Amazon.com in a new tab. From that moment you are on Amazon's site under Amazon's Privacy Notice, not this one. Amazon sets its own cookies to attribute the visit to this site's Associates tag, which is how a commission is recorded. This site does not receive your identity, your order, or your payment details from Amazon. It receives aggregate reports: clicks, and orders if any, by day and by product.

Legal basis and your choices

Because no personal data is collected, there is nothing to delete, export, or correct. If a collector is configured later and a lawful basis becomes necessary, this notice will state it before the collector is switched on. You can block the script entirely with a content blocker and the site will work identically.

Children

This site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

Changes

Any material change to this notice gets a new date at the top and a note on the flagged page.

Contact

This is a static site with no contact form, precisely so that it collects nothing. Corrections and questions are handled through the repository that publishes it.
