When you click through to a retailer

Clicking a product link takes you to a retailer, which is a different site with its own privacy policy and its own cookies. This site does not send any identifier with that click. The retailer will know you arrived from here, because that is how affiliate attribution works.

What changes when analytics is switched on

This page will be replaced, not appended to, on the day an analytics endpoint or third-party product is enabled. S02 requires accurate disclosure of cookies, pixels and third-party technologies, and a page that says "no cookies" after a cookie has been added is worse than no page at all. The decision is tracked as Q-05.

Contact

There is no contact form, because a form would need a mailbox and a privacy notice of its own. Use the repository issue tracker linked from the footer of the Flagged for Review page.
