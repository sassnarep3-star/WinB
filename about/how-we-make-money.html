How this site makes money

A plain answer, above the fold, on its own page — in addition to the disclosure strip that appears on every page of this site including this one.

As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. If you click a product link on this site and buy something on Amazon, this site may receive a commission. You pay the same price either way.

What the commission does not affect

  • The order products appear in, where an order exists, is by decision — drawer shape, capacity, whether it replaces an appliance.
  • No commission rate is published anywhere on this site, because none was verified for these products.
  • Trade-off sections are written whether or not they help a sale. The three air fryers all have a "not for" line.

What we cannot tell you

Whether these links currently track. None of the five supplied links carried a visible Associates tag when checked on 2026-11-20, and the tracking status is unresolved and published as FLG-003. If the links do not track, the site earns nothing — and you would still pay the same price, which is the only part that matters to a buyer.

What we do not do

  • No display ads.
  • No sponsored posts.
  • No paid placement.
  • No email capture without a clear purpose.
  • No cookies, and no third-party scripts that set them.
