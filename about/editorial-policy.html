About · Editorial policy

Three labels, and why they are not negotiable

Every factual statement on this site is one of three things. If a sentence cannot be assigned to one of the three, it does not get published.

LabelWhat it meansWhere it comes fromWhat it permits
ObservedWe opened the page and read this value on a dated visit.Our own visit on 2026-11-20Stating the ASIN, the category path, the listing title, and the shape or compartment count as written in the title.
Seller claimThe listing or manufacturer asserts this. We are repeating the assertion, not confirming it.The product listing textSaying "the listing says one second" — never "one-second readings".
Editorial inferenceOur reasoning from the specifications, clearly not a measurement.The editorial deskSaying two baskets mean more washing-up, because that follows from the parts count.

The words we do not use

Any phrase that claims personal experience of a product, any we found, best-in-class, and any superlative not attached to a named source. Every one of these implies evidence the desk does not have.

Corrections

If a listing changes, the observation date on the product table is wrong and should be corrected rather than quietly updated. The rule is: change the date, and if the change altered a recommendation, say so on the page.

Independence

No manufacturer paid for placement, supplied a product, or reviewed a draft. There is no sponsored content on this site other than affiliate links, which are disclosed on every page. If that ever changes, it will appear first on the Flagged for Review page.
