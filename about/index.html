About

Who writes this, and why it is shaped like this

Counterwise is a single-desk editorial project about a small number of kitchen tools. It exists because most buying advice for these products is either a specification table with no judgement or a review of something the writer never opened.

The desk

Counterwise is written and maintained as a single-desk editorial project. The desk does not operate a test kitchen; every recommendation here is a specification and fit judgement drawn from manufacturer-published listing data, not a hands-on review.

That constraint is stated on every product page rather than buried here, because it changes how you should read them. A specification judgement can tell you whether two 3 qt baskets will frustrate you; it cannot tell you whether the coating flakes after eight months. The first is useful and checkable. The second is what everyone else is already guessing at.

Why only five products

Because the five supplied form a coherent set: three air fryers that are structurally different machines, and two thermometers built for different jobs. Expanding into a twelfth category before the first two convert would produce a directory, not a shop. That decision is written down in the Decision Room with its reasoning and its downside.

How the site is built

Static HTML generated from a small data file, served by GitHub Pages. No database, no framework, no third-party script, no cookie set by the site’s own code. The sitemap and RSS feed are regenerated by the same build that writes the pages, so a new guide cannot be published without appearing in both. The build also runs the R1–R10 compliance gate, a link crawl and an HTTP smoke test at the real /WinB/ path, and fails the publish if any of them fails.

You can read the registers that drive it: the Data Room holds the product-intent record, the source register, the automation pipeline, the organic playbook and the measurement schema; the Flagged for Review page holds everything that could not be confirmed.

Brand, seasons and clusters

The brand is Counterwise. The shop is organised as two clusters — air fryers and thermometers — that link to each other and to the comparison table. A seasonal notebook on the front page reframes the guides by cooking moment as hypotheses. Imagery is generated editorial illustration; no Amazon product photography is stored, for licence reasons stated in How this site works.
