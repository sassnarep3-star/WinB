How this site works

A short, specific account of where the information on these pages comes from, and what it cannot tell you.

What we do

Counterwise compares kitchen appliances using the specifications their manufacturers publish, plus public guidance from the USDA and the FTC. We put the deciding specification at the top of each page, name the trade-off the machine makes, and link every fact back to the page it came from.

What we do not do

We have not cooked with, measured, or handled any product on this site. No page claims otherwise, and each review page repeats the point above its specification table. That makes these pages a specification comparison, not a test report, and the difference matters.

We do not show prices, star ratings, or review counts. Amazon permits them only when Amazon serves the data or it arrives through the Product Advertising API with a timestamp and disclaimer. Showing a price we cannot keep current would be both a policy breach and a disservice.

How products are ordered

The ordering is editorial and based on how often a given trade-off comes up for a household of two to four people. It is not a sales ranking, not a commission ranking, and not a quality ranking. If two products are close, the page says so rather than manufacturing a winner.

How the money works

Product links are Amazon Associates links. If you click one and buy, Amazon may pay this site a commission at no extra cost to you. That does not change the ordering, and it does not change which products are included. The full statement is on the disclosure page and at the top of every page on this site.

What is still open

Quite a lot, and it is all written down:

  • 6 flagged items — unresolved observations, each with a source.
  • 12 open questions — what must be answered before the next decision.
  • 24 sources — every document used, with retrieval dates.
  • 8 suggestions — proposed changes with their trade-offs.

Corrections

If a specification is wrong, it came from a listing that has since changed or from a reading error. The source link on each row is there so you can check it yourself. Corrections are welcome and will be reflected on the source register with a new retrieval date.
