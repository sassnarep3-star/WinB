FLG-001 Medium Product identity Open

The five submitted links do not encode an ASIN

Observed. Each submitted link is a 9-character opaque token such as B04G1MEwY. An Amazon ASIN is a 10-character alphanumeric identifier. The token therefore cannot be read as an ASIN, and none of the five tokens matches the ASIN its link actually resolves to.

Missing. The submitter's intended product record for each link.

What would close it. Confirm the intended ASIN for each link inside the enrolled Associates workflow and record it separately from the observed redirect target.

FLG-003 High Link mechanics Open

Tracking tag propagation through the supplied short links was not observed

Observed. Resolving the five submitted short links on 2026-11-20 produced Amazon product URLs carrying session-scoped parameters (lv, btn_type, btn_ref, channelId, plpRedirect). No tag parameter appeared in the resolved URLs. This is an observation about the redirect, not a finding that the links are invalid or non-compliant; their exact status is unresolved.

Missing. An Associates click-report check confirming whether a click on each short link is attributed to the tracking ID.

What would close it. One click test per product, then a look at the Associates click report.
