The pattern

What the questions have in common

  • Almost every gap is closed by something only the account owner can produce: the product record, the click report, the Commission Income Statement, or the approved integration.
  • None of them is closed by writing more. The guide cluster is not blocked by effort; it is blocked by evidence.
  • Four of them — Q-02, Q-03, Q-06 and Q-08 — are about money and measurement, and until they close, the site publishes no earnings figure and no performance claim at all.
