Scope and provenance

How this register is built

  • The five submitted links and the tracking ID came from the project brief. They are recorded verbatim and are not treated as product truth.
  • Every product fact was read directly from the official Amazon listing on 2026-11-20 and is labelled with that date.
  • Every policy statement links to the publisher's own page, with the update date shown where the page shows one.
  • Guide content is general editorial guidance and avoids claims about named models beyond what the linked listing states.
  • Where a value could not be confirmed from an official source, the cell is blank and the item is published on the Flagged for Review page.
