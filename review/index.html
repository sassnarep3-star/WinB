Disclosure & policy review gates

The disclosure strip appears above the content on every page of this build and again in the footer, using the exact sentence required by Operating Agreement §5. Every outbound product link is generated with rel="sponsored" by the builder — it cannot be forgotten page-by-page. On every push, CI runs scripts/validate_site.py as a hard gate: disclosure on 100% of pages, sponsored-rel coverage, internal-link integrity, sitemap/feed sync, register schema (including the intended-vs-observed separation), external-domain whitelist, and a rebuild-diff check that committed HTML matches the sources byte-for-byte. A failing gate blocks deploy; that is the entire point.
