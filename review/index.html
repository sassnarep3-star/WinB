Disclosure & policy gates

The disclosure strip sits directly under the masthead on every page and is repeated in the footer; the CI gate fails the build if either is missing or moved. Every outbound product link is rel="sponsored nofollow" and built as amazon.com/dp/<ASIN>?tag=shopnow047c-20 — one Special Link per product. Before adding or scaling links, re-read the current official terms:

  • Amazon Associates Program Operating Agreement — official Amazon program source; review the current version and applicable marketplace terms.
  • Amazon Associates Program Policies — official Amazon source; read 2026-11-20 (page: updated 2026-04-14).
  • FTC: Endorsement Guides, What People Are Asking — official U.S. FTC guidance; read 2026-11-20.
  • FTC: Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers — official FTC disclosure resource.
  • Google: Qualify your outbound links — rel="sponsored" definition; read 2026-11-20.

These are review starting points, not legal advice or a claim of completed compliance review. Endorsement context stays a human review step before each campaign (Q10 on the direction page).
