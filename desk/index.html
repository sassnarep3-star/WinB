One page for the whole operation: what exists, what was verified today, what is blocked, and which decisions move it forward. Reader-facing pages live upstairs; this is the machine room.

Status as of 2026-11-20

  1. The site is a shop now, not an audit. The front page and shop lead with the five products; verification lives one click away in the register. Every page carries the disclosure strip above the content and again in the footer — the exact Operating Agreement §5 sentence.
  2. All five submitted links were resolved the same day (anonymous fetch). Destinations: three air fryers (Chefman dual-basket 6 qt, Emeril Lagasse French-door 26 qt, Chefman 8 qt XL) and two thermometers (ThermoMaven instant-read, CHEF iQ Sense Gen 3 probe set). Loud caveat: every redirect carried a fallback signature (plpRedirect=mhFallback) and matched none of the tokens embedded in the submitted links.
  3. No numbers we didn't see are published. Zero prices, ratings, traffic, or commission figures on the site. Maker pages corroborate two of five product families (chefman.com, chefiq.com); the Emeril and ThermoMaven manufacturer domains remain unidentified and are flagged.
  4. Automation is real but gated. scripts/build.py + scripts/validate_site.py run in CI as the publish gate; the click-event emitter ships inert until an ingestion endpoint exists; and per Amazon's official deprecation notice, product-data automation is planned against the Creators API, not the retired PA-API 5.
  5. The compliance spine is structural. Sponsored rels are emitted by the builder (not hand-added), external domains are whitelisted by the validator, and the build-check itself fails if committed HTML ever diverges from sources — “blank means unverified” is enforced against the site, not just promised by it.

Measurement & automation status

Defined events: page_view, outbound_click, guide_engaged, disclosure_view, affiliate_conversion_reported. The client emitter (assets/js/site.js) is inert while the endpoint meta tag is blank — no cookies, no fingerprinting, no silent buffering. Provisioning ingestion is S-04; conversions only ever arrive from program reports, never inferred from clicks.

Publishing cadence: one guide per week; the build regenerates sitemap + feed, so a new guide is one JSON row, one HTML body, and python3 scripts/build.py.

The five decisions that unblock everything

  1. Confirm link destinations & rebuild them as Special Links (S-01) — gates monetization.
  2. Confirm Associates account standing + this domain registered (Q-03) — gates attribution.
  3. Pick the price/ratings data path (S-02: dated capture or Creators API) — gates comparison tables.
  4. Provision the analytics endpoint (S-04) — gates measurement; the emitter waits, inert and honest.
  5. Decide the authorship stance (S-09: desk bylines vs named human) — gates any “we tested” language, permanently.
