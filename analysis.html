Analysis & findings

What the verified register tells us, and what it doesn't. Every row links to an official source; anything that can't be confirmed is flagged, not filled.

Finding 1

The five links resolve into two categories.

Following each submitted link to its official listing shows the products split cleanly into air fryers and meat thermometers. The two categories share a "Kitchen & Dining" parent but sit in different Amazon browse nodes — so content, comparisons and commission treatment are best kept separate.

Finding 2

Category structure — read from official breadcrumbs.

The category path for each product was read from the official listing's breadcrumb. These are the nodes the content should be organized around.

Finding 3

Commission is category-based and must be read, not assumed.

Amazon Associates commission is set by category and is defined in the Operating Agreement. No specific percentage is stated here because the current rate schedule was not fetched from an official source in this session — it is left to be confirmed from Associates Central.

Finding 4

What we deliberately did not do.

No ASIN guessing

Submitted 9-char tokens are not ASINs. The 10-char codes shown are observed redirect targets, measured on the live listing.

No price inflation

Price/rating fields are blank and flagged rather than filled with inferred values.

No revenue forecast

The campaign ledger starts empty. No conversion score or earnings projection is presented.

No unverified claims

Working titles are labeled as working titles — not popularity or superiority claims.
