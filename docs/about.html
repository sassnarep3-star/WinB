Executive summary

What COUNTER is, and how it's built.

COUNTER is an Amazon Associates affiliate marketing site for kitchen products — air fryers and meat thermometers. It is built to look like a polished, publication-style shop front that earns organic traffic, with every product verified against an official Amazon listing and every outbound link disclosed and marked as sponsored.

The goal

Collect, organize and present a product catalog a buyer can act on — reading like a shop, not an audit. A careful source register is turned into pages a buyer can use, findable by search engines, with the machinery (verification, measurement, compliance) kept trustworthy behind a clean front.

The products — five, verified, in two categories

Five submitted link.amazon links were resolved live on 2026-11-20. Each redirects to an official Amazon listing; the resolved ASIN, title, brand and category were read from the official listing. The register splits into two categories:

  • Air Fryers — CHEFMAN 6 Quart Dual Basket Air Fryer Oven, Emeril Lagasse 26QT French Door Air Fryer Toaster Oven, Chefman 8 Qt TurboFry.
  • Meat Thermometers — ThermoMaven 1 Sec Professional Digital Meat Thermometer, CHEF iQ Sense Smart Wireless Meat Thermometer (Gen3).

Every row links to its official Amazon listing. Price, rating and review count are left blank and flagged rather than inferred — see Flagged for Review.

The verification-first approach

  • No ASIN guessing. Submitted 9-character codes are short-link tokens, not ASINs (Amazon defines an ASIN as 10 characters). The 10-character codes on the site are the observed redirect targets, measured by following each link.
  • Intent and observation kept separate. A product-intent confirmation record stores the intended ASIN, listing URL, confirmation date and source in separate fields from the observed redirect target. Intent fields are blank until the owner confirms.
  • No hallucinated values. Anything that can't be confirmed from an official source is left blank and flagged, with the source link provided for manual review.

Compliance as a gate

The required Associates statement — “As an Amazon Associate, COUNTER earns from qualifying purchases.” — appears in a disclosure strip under the masthead on every page and in the footer. Every outbound Amazon link is marked as sponsored (rel="sponsored"). Disclosure placement and endorsement context are reviewed against the Amazon Associates Operating Agreement and the FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR Part 255). Official sources are linked for manual review on the Compliance page.

Measurement before optimization

A privacy-preserving outbound-click counter and a defined set of five tracking events are in place, with a campaign ledger that starts empty. The ledger records timestamp, source, channel, eligible sessions, clicks and reported orders — populated only from authorized first-party reports, with a dated provenance trail. No conversion score or revenue forecast is presented.

Decision-driven, not instinct-driven

A dedicated Suggestions section lists proposed improvements (priority, what it does, why) plus the open questions, information and answers that must be settled before any decision is reasonably made. Missing information is flagged and the dependent decision stays open until it's obtained.

Build & publish

The site is a static, multi-page site (clean dashboard-style UI, tables for all entries and findings) deployed to GitHub Pages from the /docs directory, with a sitemap, robots file and canonical links so search engines have one site to index. The build was verified line by line and all internal links checked before the pull request was merged.
