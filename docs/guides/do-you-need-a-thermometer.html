Do you need a meat thermometer if you own an air fryer?

Air fryers brown the outside before the inside is done. A thermometer is the only way to know which happened.

Why time alone fails

Air frying is a high-surface-heat method. The outside can look finished while the centre is well behind. A recipe's time is a starting point tuned to a specific thickness and a specific machine.

Judging by colour works for vegetables and fails for poultry and pork, where the outside browns long before the centre is safe.

Instant-read versus leave-in wireless

An instant-read is a handheld probe you insert at the end to check. It is cheap, needs no app, and works everywhere. It only reads where you put it, so you check piece by piece.

A wireless probe stays in the food and reports continuously. It suits long cooks where you want to walk away, and it removes the open-the-door-and-lose-heat loop. It brings an app, a pairing step, and extra parts.

What to check before trusting a stated accuracy

A headline accuracy figure is only meaningful with a temperature range attached. A probe accurate to half a degree at room temperature may be less accurate near 400°F, which is where air frying happens.

Look for a stated range in the manual rather than a single number in the title. Calibration instructions are a good sign that the maker expects the reading to matter.

The practical test

Fill a glass with crushed ice and water, stir, and insert the probe. A properly calibrated thermometer reads close to freezing. If it is well off, the reading you take from dinner is unreliable too.

This takes a minute and tells you more than any specification.

If you only buy one

Buy the instant-read unless you specifically cook long roasts and want to monitor without opening the door. It covers the job that matters most in an air fryer, for the least money and the fewest dependencies.

The checklist.
  • Do you cook poultry or pork where colour is not a safe guide?
  • Short checks or long monitored cooks?
  • Accuracy figure with a stated temperature range
  • Calibration method published by the maker
  • App required, and what it retains
