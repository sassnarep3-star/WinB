The four questions that actually choose for you

1. What do you cook most?

Two foods finishing together → dual basket. Racks, toast, and sheet-pan dinners → oven style. One big crispy batch → big drawer.

2. What does your counter allow?

Measure width, depth, and door clearance. An oven you can't fully open is an oven you'll stop using. (Cleanup & space guide)

3. How many people, how often?

Capacity checklists beat quart claims. (Capacity guide)

4. Who watches the doneness?

If you cook by instinct alone, an instant-read is the upgrade. If you host, a wireless probe protects the whole evening. (Thermometer guide)
