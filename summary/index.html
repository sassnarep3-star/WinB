What this is

A short, honest version

The brief. Five Amazon short links and a tracking ID, with a goal of automating product research, publishing SEO-optimised articles and measuring performance. No product identities, specifications, prices, reviews, eligibility evidence or reporting data were supplied with them.

What was verified. All five links were resolved and read against their official Amazon listings on 2026-11-20. Five products are now tracked with their stated specifications, observed prices, ratings, review counts, availability and sellers. Fifteen alternative listings were captured from Amazon's own recommendation module. The Amazon Associates Operating Agreement, Program Policies, Participation Requirements, application FAQ and the Creators API documentation were read directly, along with the FTC endorsement guidance.

What was built. A static site generated from a single data layer, with 9 question-shaped guides, two category hubs, a seven-page workbench covering products, competition, content, links, campaigns and compliance, and a review section holding the evidence register, 12 open questions, 24 suggestions, 14 flagged items and a source index. A verifier runs on every build and blocks the deploy if a table row loses its source link, a page loses its disclosure, an outbound link stops being marked sponsored, or an internal link breaks.

What was deliberately not done. No product is called the best. No commission rate, click count, order, conversion rate or earnings figure appears anywhere, because none has been obtained from an authorised first-party report. The campaign ledger is empty by design. No search volume or trend claim is made, and every working title is labelled a hypothesis.

The five things that matter most

If you read nothing else

  1. Identity is confirmed by observation, not by intent. Each short link resolves to a specific Amazon listing, and that redirect is recorded in its own field. The intended ASIN is left blank because the submitted tokens are not ASINs and no enrolled-workflow confirmation exists. FLG-001
  2. The published links are not the supplied links. The site uses the canonical /dp/<ASIN>?tag=shopnow047c-20 form with rel="sponsored" and a visible Sponsored marker. The supplied short links appear only in the evidence register. FLG-003
  3. No money number exists yet. Commission rates, eligibility, orders and earnings all require the account's own reports. Until they arrive, the campaign ledger is empty and the site publishes no revenue figure of any kind. FLG-008
  4. The catalogue API changed. Product Advertising API 5.0 is deprecated and now returns HTTP 403; the Creators API is the supported successor. Any automation plan must be built against the Creators API. FLG-011
  5. Disclosure is a build gate, not a habit. Every page carries the strip under the masthead and the notice in the footer, and the verifier fails the deploy if either disappears. FLG-002
