The one-link test (before any link scales)

Per the brief and Amazon's own guidance: for each product, use the enrolled Associates workflow to test one link, confirm it lands on the intended destination, and confirm the click appears in the tracking report. Results go in the intent record. Until then, the submitted short links remain exactly as supplied, marked sponsored, with their compliance status recorded as unresolved — we do not claim they are invalid (FLG-003).
