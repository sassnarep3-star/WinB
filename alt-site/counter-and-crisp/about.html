About

A buying desk with its receipts attached.

Counter & Crisp launched on 2026-11-20 with five products on the desk and one rule: if a number can't be tied to an official source, it doesn't go on the page.

Editorial & verification policy

  • Sources or silence. Every register row links an official source or is blank and flagged. We never infer, estimate, or round into a cell.
  • Seller claims stay claims. Capacities, certifications, and accuracies copied from listing titles are labelled as the seller's statements.
  • Rankings are not for sale. We earn from qualifying purchases through marked links; commission rates do not influence order or wording.
  • Disclosures above the fold. The disclosure strip sits under the masthead of every page and repeats in the footer — the statement required by the Associates Operating Agreement is verbatim on both.
  • Corrections. An error found later is corrected in the register with a dated note — not silently overwritten.

Authorship

Posts are signed "the Counter & Crisp Desk" for now. This is a deliberate placeholder: a named editor with real credentials materially improves trust, and we will not invent one. The proposal to attach a real person is open as SUG-008.

Imagery

The photography on this site is commissioned editorial work — it is not product photography, and it is never presented as evidence about a specific product. Product images will come from licensed sources only (see SUG-004).

Seasons & clusters

Content ships in topical clusters (air fryers; thermometers) and seasonal edits (currently the Holiday 2026 edit). The rotation is proposed as a standing calendar in SUG-001.

Privacy, briefly

No cookies, no personal data, no third-party trackers. The click counter is privacy-preserving and documented in full on measurement.

The machinery is part of the product

Every dashboard surface — the register, compliance gate, measurement, flags, suggestions, and open questions — lives one click away in the footer. The shop leads; the evidence follows.
