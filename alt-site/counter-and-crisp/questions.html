Questions, answers, and what's missing.

A decision is only as good as the information behind it. This register lists what we know (with sources), what we don't (with a discovery path), and which decisions each open item blocks. It exists so nobody has to guess what's missing — including us.

Answered — with official sources

IDQuestionAnswerSource
Q-01What disclosure must an Amazon Associate site display?The Operating Agreement requires the statement “As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.” to appear clearly and conspicuously on the site; link-level disclosures must be clear and conspicuous as well.SRC-05; SRC-06
Q-02What does the FTC require of affiliate recommendations?Endorsements must reflect honest opinions, and material connections (like commissions) must be disclosed clearly and conspicuously where the recommendation appears.SRC-01; SRC-02; SRC-12
Q-03What is the documented shape of an Amazon ASIN?Amazon's developer glossary defines ASINs as unique blocks of 10 letters and/or numbers.SRC-07
Q-04What internal temperatures are safe for meats?USDA FSIS chart: steaks/chops/roasts 145 °F + 3 min rest; ground meats 160 °F; all poultry 165 °F; fish 145 °F; leftovers 165 °F.SRC-03
Q-05Where can a buyer check appliance recalls?CPSC's Recalls & Product Safety Warnings database is searchable and lists appliance categories including Air Fryer.SRC-11
