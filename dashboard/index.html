What the register does not contain

  • No prices. Amazon permits price display only when Amazon serves the link containing the data, or when it arrives through the Product Advertising API with a required date/time stamp and disclaimer. Neither applies, so every price cell is empty rather than stale. FLG-002
  • No star ratings or review counts. Same rule: customer reviews and ratings may be displayed only when obtained through the Creators API or PA API.
  • No Best Seller Rank, sales volume, or conversion rate. None of these appear on a product page, and none may be invented.
  • No keyword volumes. No search-volume source has been consulted in this pass, so no demand figure appears anywhere.
  • No conversion or revenue forecast. Only Amazon's own reports can supply orders. The campaign ledger starts empty and stays empty until a first-party report fills it.
