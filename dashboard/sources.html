Sources deliberately not used

  • Affiliate blogs and how-to sites. Several rank well and restate Amazon's commission rules. Restating a rule is not the rule; only Associates Central was used for programme policy.
  • Keyword and commission calculators. No search-volume or rate figure from a third-party tool is reproduced, because none can be verified against a first-party source here.
  • Cached or archived copies. Policies change. Only live publisher pages were read.

Re-verification

Amazon's Operating Agreement and Program Policies both carry update dates, and the rate schedule can change without notice. Re-open each policy link and the five product links on the first of each month, then update the "Retrieved" column. A stale register is more dangerous than an empty one.
