Why this page exists rather than an answer

Several of these questions can only be answered from inside an Amazon Associates account or from a Search Console property, neither of which is reachable from here. Writing a plausible answer into the site would make it look finished and be wrong. The register keeps the gap visible so the next decision is made on evidence.

Money

Q-01 Is tracking ID shopnow047c-20 enrolled and in good standing today?

We have
The ID matches the documented US Store ID pattern (-20 suffix).
We do not have
Account status, approval date, and whether the three-qualifying-sales window is open or closed.
What it blocks
Every revenue decision. Nothing else matters if the account cannot be paid.
How to get it
Sign in to Associates Central, Account Settings, and record the status with a date.
Linked flag
FLG-005

Q-02 Do the five supplied short links actually attribute clicks to that ID?

We have
All five resolve to the correct product pages.
We do not have
A click through one link, followed by its appearance in the Associates click report.
What it blocks
Whether these products can be promoted at all, or must be re-linked.
How to get it
One controlled test click per product, checked against the reports, before scaling.
Linked flag
FLG-003

Q-03 Which commission category applies, and at what rate?

We have
All five products sit under Home & Kitchen, in the Air Fryers and Meat Thermometers nodes.
We do not have
The current rate for those product groups in the Standard Commission Income Statement, read while signed in.
What it blocks
Any comparison of product value per click. Rates change, so an unverified rate is worse than none.
How to get it
Open the Commission Income Statement while signed in and record the rate plus the date read.
Linked flag
FLG-006

Content

Q-04 Do we test products ourselves, or permanently publish as a specification comparison?

A complete specification comparison pipeline fed by listing copy.

Every word of review copy. The two answers produce different sites.

Measurement

Q-07 What is the difference between an outbound click and a sale?

We have
A defined five-event model: page_view, outbound_click, product_click, guide_complete, subscribe.
We do not have
The link between any of those events and an actual reported order.
What it blocks
Any conversion rate claim. Only Amazon's own reports can supply orders.
How to get it
Reconcile this site's outbound click counts against Associates click reports weekly.

Q-08 Where will the analytics events actually go?

We have
A privacy-preserving client that sends a fixed event name and a page path, nothing else.
We do not have
A deployed endpoint. The endpoint slot is empty by design and the client no-ops until it is set.
What it blocks
Any measured iteration. Without a collector there is no data, only opinion.
How to get it
Deploy a self-hosted collector and set one constant in assets/analytics.js.

Compliance

Q-09 Does the disclosure satisfy both Amazon and the FTC in its current placement?

We have
The exact required statement, above the fold under the masthead on every page, plus a footer repeat and a per-link 'sponsored' label.
We do not have
A read of the live site against 16 CFR 255.0(f) by someone accountable for it.
What it blocks
Nothing technically. It is a judgement call that should be recorded, not assumed.
How to get it
Walk the rendered pages once and note where the statement appears relative to each link.

Q-10 What does the privacy policy have to say about the click counter?

We have
A policy page stating that events are aggregate, contain no identifiers, and set no cookies.
We do not have
Confirmation from whoever runs the deployed collector that this stays true, and any state-law obligations triggered by it.
What it blocks
Turning the counter on.
How to get it
