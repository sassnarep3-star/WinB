Ledger rules

The rules that keep it honest

  1. A row may only be added when the figure came from an authorised first-party report. A number estimated, modelled or remembered does not belong here.
  2. Every row carries a dated provenance trail: who produced the report, when, and where it came from.
  3. Reported orders are entered only from the account's own commission report. A click is never promoted to an order.
  4. No conversion score, click-through rate, revenue forecast or earnings projection is derived from this ledger and published on this site.
  5. Rows may be corrected, never silently overwritten. A correction adds a dated note to the row.
