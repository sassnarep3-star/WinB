Measurement

Five defined events, a privacy-preserving client, and a campaign ledger that starts empty. No conversion score, revenue forecast, or projected traffic figure appears here, because none of those can be produced without data this site does not have.

The collector is not connected

The client below is complete and loaded on every page. Its endpoint is a single empty constant, so today it records nothing. That is deliberate: shipping a silent no-op is honest, whereas shipping a fake dashboard would not be. Setting the endpoint is Q-08.

What the numbers can and cannot say

  • A click is not a sale. This site can count an outbound click. Only Amazon's Associates reports can confirm that a purchase was attributed, after the 24-hour session window closes and returns are accounted for.
  • No conversion rate is shown. A conversion rate needs orders in the numerator. Until the ledger has a reported-orders column with real values, any rate would be a guess dressed as a metric.
  • No revenue forecast. Commission depends on the product category rate, on qualifying revenue after shipping and tax exclusions, and on returns. None of those are known here.
  • Attribution to a specific visitor is prohibited. Amazon's Participation Requirements forbid associating a sub-tag or reporting identifier with a specific end user, which is one reason this client sends no identifier at all.

Privacy posture of the counter

  • No cookies. No localStorage. No fingerprinting. No third-party script.
  • The event name, the page path, and the ASIN of the product clicked. That is the whole payload.
  • No IP address is stored by the client, and the design intent is that the collector truncates or discards it.
  • Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control are honoured: if either is set, nothing is sent.
