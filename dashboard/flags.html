Five product links resolve correctly and none of them carried a tracking tag when checked. That is the single largest open item and it is a revenue problem, not a publishing problem. It sits here, dated, rather than being quietly re-linked or quietly ignored.

FLG-003 High unresolved

The five supplied short links carry no Associates tag

Observed. Each supplied link.amazon/<id> URL was fetched on 2026-11-20. All five returned a product detail page on www.amazon.com. None of the observed redirect targets carried an Associates tag parameter such as tag=shopnow047c-20.

Why it matters. Amazon pays commission only on clicks through Special Links that carry your Associates tag (Program Policies, Participation Requirements §2(b)). A link without the tag can still send the shopper to the right product, but the purchase is not attributed to this account.

What is not claimed. This register does not claim the supplied short links are invalid or non-compliant. Their exact compliance status is unresolved, because link.amazon is an Amazon-operated shortener whose own redirect behaviour could add attribution outside the URL we observed.

Next step. Verify one link inside an enrolled Associates account: click it, then confirm the click appears in the Associates Central reports for tracking ID shopnow047c-20 before scaling to all five.

Sources.

  • Amazon.com Associates Central — Associates Program Policies
  • Amazon.com Associates Central — Associates Program Operating Agreement
  • Amazon.com — Amazon Associates Central FAQ (Account, Store ID, Payments, Product Advertising API)
FLG-002 High policy-driven

No prices, star ratings, or review counts are published

Observed. Prices and ratings exist on the Amazon listings but are deliberately absent from every page of this site.

Why it matters. Program Policies, Participation Requirements §2(b), allow a site to show price and availability only if Amazon serves the link containing that data, or if the data is obtained through the Creators API or PA API with the required date/time stamp and disclaimer. Neither condition is met here, so any price shown would breach the Operating Agreement and would also go stale.

What is not claimed. No price, discount, or savings figure anywhere on this site is presented as current.

Next step. If price display is wanted later, enrol in the Product Advertising API and render prices only from API responses with the required timestamp and disclaimer.

Sources.

  • Amazon.com Associates Central — Associates Program Policies
FLG-001 High open

Nothing here has been hands-on tested

Observed. No named person has used, measured, or cooked with any of the five products for this site. Product attributes come from the Amazon listing text only.

Why it matters. FTC guidance states plainly that you cannot talk about your experience with a product you have not tried, and that endorsements must reflect honest opinions (Disclosures 101; 16 CFR 255.1). A publication that implies testing it did not do is exposing the operator, not just the brand.

What is not claimed. No page claims testing, measuring, or ownership. Claims drawn from listing copy are labelled as such.

Next step. Either buy and document one unit per category, or keep the 'we compare published specifications' framing permanent and visible on every review page.

Sources.

  • Federal Trade Commission — Disclosures 101 for Social Media Influencers
  • Federal Trade Commission (eCFR) — 16 CFR Part 255 — Guides Concerning Use of Endorsements and Testimonials in Advertising
  • Federal Trade Commission — FTC's Endorsement Guides: What People Are Asking
FLG-005 Medium unresolved

Tracking ID enrolment cannot be confirmed from outside the account

Observed. The requested tracking ID, shopnow047c-20, follows the documented US Store ID pattern, which ends in -20.

Why it matters. Whether that Store ID is currently enrolled, in good standing, or past the three-qualifying-sales review window is visible only inside Associates Central. A site can be perfectly compliant and still earn nothing if the account is withdrawn.

What is not claimed. This site does not assert that shopnow047c-20 is active or approved.

FLG-004 Low open

Manufacturer certification and range claims are unverified

Observed. Listing titles for the two thermometers state 'NIST&NSF Certified', '±0.5°F', 'WiFi Unlimited Range', and '1000°F Heat Safe'.

Why it matters. These are the manufacturer's own listing claims. Repeating a certification claim without checking a certifying body's record is the kind of detail that turns a helpful page into a liability.

What is not claimed. The site attributes these phrases to the listing and does not restate them as independent findings.

Next step. Check the NSF product listing database and a NIST-traceable calibration certificate for each thermometer before repeating the claims in body copy.
