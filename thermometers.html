Category · Thermometers

Thermometers: end the guesswork.

One tool answers 'is it done?' in a second; the other watches the whole cook from across the kitchen. Both beat cutting into the roast to look.

What to compare in this category

  • Instant-read vs leave-in: spot-checks versus continuous monitoring. Many kitchens eventually want both.
  • Speed & probe length: thin, fast probes read without leaving a hole; long probes reach the center of big roasts.
  • Accuracy claims need evidence: when a listing claims certification, ask for the documentation (our Q-12).
  • The number that matters is USDA's: poultry 165 °F, ground meats 160 °F, steaks 145 °F + 3 min rest (USDA FSIS chart).
  • Heat limits: a probe rated for the grill grate is not the same as one rated for the broiler. Check the listing's stated limit.
