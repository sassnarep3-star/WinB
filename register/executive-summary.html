Executive summary

As of 2026-11-20. What this site covers, what is verified, and what is not.

What the site is

A product register for five Amazon products from the links you supplied: three air fryers and two meat thermometers. It is published inside the Counterwise site under /register/ and linked from the navigation and footer of every page. The register lists each product with its listing title, observed ASIN, link status and source, and records every policy rule that applies.

What is verified

  • Each supplied short link resolves to one Amazon product page. The five ASINs seen on those pages are B0CDY277D3, B09B7SB46R, B08DKYBTPH, B0DHZTCNR5 and B0DZFD6R9Z (confirmation record).
  • Amazon's Operating Agreement §5 requires the statement "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.". It is on every page.
  • Amazon's Program Policies limit how prices and ratings may be shown, and bar price tracking unless Amazon agrees (Compliance).
  • FTC guidance and Google's rel="sponsored" guidance are applied (Compliance).

What is not verified

  • The intended ASIN for each link. The short-link codes are 9 characters, so they cannot be read as ASINs. The field is left blank.
  • Whether the supplied short links comply with Program Policies §6(w), and whether they attribute clicks to the tracking ID shopnow047c-20. A test click through the Associates account is needed.
  • Prices and ratings. They are not shown (Flagged for review, FLG-05).
  • The five analytics events, the author byline, and the brand's final name.

What we recommend deciding first

  1. How to validate the links (D1). Validate one per product before scaling.
  2. Whether to pursue price and rating data, which requires API access, or stay link-only (D2).
  3. How clicks will be measured (D3). Associates reports are the simplest start.

Nothing has been changed on the site to act on these choices. Each one is listed in Suggestions with its options.

Irregularities and integration notes

The Counterwise 'Evidence & review' page said no product had been matched, because the links had not been resolved. This register resolved them and recorded the Amazon product page each one reaches, with the intended ASIN still blank (FLG-19). The reader sign-up form on the front page has no backend (FLG-20), and the analytics client sends nothing until an endpoint is configured (FLG-21).

22 items are on the Flagged for review page with source links. None blocks publication, but FLG-01 to FLG-04 should be checked before the links are relied on.
