Suggestion Board

Every proposed change to this project, with the realistic paths laid side by side. Review the options, choose the best one, and record the choice — implementation starts only after that. Nothing on this page has been built because it appears here.

6 awaiting a decision 2 decided and recorded

How this board works

One. Read the proposal and why it is on the board. Two. Compare its options on effort, payoff and risk — the recommended path is marked, but it is a recommendation, not a decision. Three. Record a choice with the suggestion ID and option letter (for example, SUG-01: A) in chat or as a repository issue. Four. Only then is the change implemented, and the state on this page is updated in the same build that ships it.

The rule the board exists to enforce: choose the best path before implementing any change. A suggestion without a recorded choice stays a proposal, however loudly it is recommended.

What this board deliberately does not do

It does not rank options by revenue potential, because no revenue has been measured (the campaign ledger is empty and says why). It does not score ideas with invented weights, and it does not mark anything implemented to make the list look productive. A proposal here is cheap; a decision is the expensive part, which is why the gate sits before the build and not after it.
