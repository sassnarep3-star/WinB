Growth plan

Publish on a cadence, then measure what arrives

This page is a plan, not a report card. It sets a cadence, defines what "working" would mean, and states plainly that no traffic figure, conversion rate or revenue projection exists yet — because inventing one would be the most damaging thing this site could do.

The cadence

One guide per week for eight weeks, then stop and read the data before writing a ninth.

Publishing a fixed list of eight titles now would be the mistake this plan exists to avoid. Weeks five to eight are deliberately unspecified.

These thresholds are honest guesses, set deliberately low, and labelled as guesses. They exist so that a decision can be made later without renegotiating the standard. The Associates row is the only one this site cannot measure by itself.

Findability: one site, a sitemap, and a reason to rank

The site publishes a single canonical host, sassnarep3-star.github.io/WinB, a sitemap at the root, a robots.txt that points at it, and an RSS feed of new guides. Those are the mechanical preconditions for indexing — nothing more. Google documents the sitemap formats it accepts but states no preference among them (S07), so this site ships an XML sitemap for pages plus an RSS feed for readers.

The editorial reason to rank is narrower than a keyword strategy: each guide answers a question with a decision at the end, and each names what it could not verify. That is a defensible position against a competitor publishing confident filler. Whether it ranks is a question for the measurement plan, not for this paragraph.

No search-volume claims are made anywhere in this plan

No keyword tool was consulted, so no volume, difficulty or ranking probability is stated. The four topics are working hypotheses. Treating them as validated demand would be exactly the hallucination this project is built to avoid.

The measurement prerequisite, before anything else

Turn on measurement before writing week two. The site already fires five defined events client-side, but a client-side counter with no endpoint is a note in a notebook. Until Q-05 is answered there is no way to know whether week one worked, and every subsequent decision is then made on taste. The events are defined on the metrics page.
