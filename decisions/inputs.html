The information behind every choice

What we know, what we do not, and how to find out

A decision is only reasonable when its missing column is empty. This page lists every decision currently waiting, the information already in hand, the information still absent, and a suggested way to discover the absent part. The suggestions are routes to evidence — they are not evidence themselves.

The rule this page enforces

Nothing on this page is decided. Rows marked Ready have their option sets drafted on the suggestion board; rows marked Blocked cannot be evaluated until someone with the right access runs the check named in the discovery column. Where a value could not be confirmed from an official source, the register leaves it blank and says so — the same discipline applies here.

DI-01

Can the five supplied short links be used as money links?

Blocked — needs owner access

What we have

  • All five short links resolve to live listings (resolved again 2026-11-20; same targets and listing titles as the register).
  • The resolved URLs carry tracking parameters but no tag= parameter (FLG-001).
  • Policy requires the Associate ID as a parameter on every link to Amazon (A2 §2(b)).

What is missing

  • Whether the links were minted inside the shopnow047c-20 account with the tag applied at click time.
  • A tracking report covering at least one click per product (FLG-003).

Suggested ways to discover the missing information

  1. Mint one short link inside Associates Central while signed in to shopnow047c-20, resolve it, and compare its parameters with the supplied set (Q-01).
  2. Click one link per product through the enrolled workflow and confirm the destination and the tracking report before scaling (the FLG-003 action).

Blocks: FLG-001, FLG-003 — revenue attribution, not publication · Owner: Site owner

DI-02

Which channel should produce the first 100 sessions?

Open — needs owner inventory

What we have

  • No analytics are deployed, so current traffic is unmeasured (FLG-007).
  • Search on a new project path will not answer head terms in the first month.

What is missing

  • An inventory of audiences the owner already has: a list, a following, a community.
  • Any budget or intent for paid or pinned distribution.

Suggested ways to discover the missing information

  1. Owner lists every channel they can reach today, with a rough audience size (Q-02).
  2. If the answer is none, plan for long-tail queries plus one community the owner already participates in — not a head-term bet.

Blocks: Publishing cadence, topic choice (DS-03) · Owner: Site owner

DI-03

Should the site buy a review unit to earn hands-on content?

Open — money decision

What we have

  • No unit has been handled by this desk; FTC guidance bars describing experience with untried products (FLG-004).
  • Specification-and-fit content is fully shipped and internally consistent without it.

What is missing

  • Whether either brand will supply a unit to a project with no traffic history.
  • A budget for buying the cheapest of the five and documenting the purchase.

Suggested ways to discover the missing information

  1. Ask both brands directly for a review unit and document the ask and the answer (Q-03).
  2. If the brands decline, buy the cheapest unit with a published receipt — a disclosed purchase is a legitimate basis for a test protocol.

Blocks: DS-05 imagery, hands-on reviews, differentiation · Owner: Site owner

DI-04

When does measurement go live, and what counts as a baseline?

Blocked — needs an endpoint

What we have

  • The five-event counter and click recorder ship with the site and run in the reader’s browser.
  • A deployable collector exists at the analytics path but is not running (FLG-007).
  • The campaign ledger schema is frozen and deliberately empty.

What is missing

  • A hosted endpoint — the supplied worker or any first-party alternative.
  • A decision on where events are stored and for how long.

Suggested ways to discover the missing information

  1. Deploy the supplied worker, set the endpoint value in the site script, and confirm one event arrives before trusting any number (SUG-03).
  2. Treat the first 30 days as a baseline, not a scoreboard; small samples mislead.

Blocks: Ledger rows, topic selection from measured queries, every performance claim · Owner: Site owner with the desk

DI-05

Where should captured readers go, and how is contact handled?

Open — provider choice

What we have

  • The capture form is deliberately inert and says so; it counts intent only.
  • The repository issue tracker is currently the only corrections route.

What is missing

  • A mailing provider with a documented data story (Q-04).
  • A public contact address, or a formalised issue-tracker route (Q-07).

Suggested ways to discover the missing information

  1. Pick a provider, then wire the endpoint and the consent copy in one change (DS-06).
  2. Create one public address, or publish the issue tracker as the official route and say so on the About page.

Blocks: DS-06, audience building, the weekly reader-question idea (SUG-07) · Owner: Site owner

DI-06

Who is the publication — a desk or a named editor — and on what domain?

Open — identity decision

What we have

  • Pages sign as “The Counterwise desk” with a stated non-testing policy.
  • The site runs as a GitHub Pages project path with a full sitemap and feed.

What is missing

  • Whether the owner will publish a real byline (Q-05).
  • Whether a custom domain will be bought, and who controls DNS (Q-06).
  • A Search Console verification token from the owner’s Google account.

Suggested ways to discover the missing information

  1. Owner decision on the byline; a named author plus a two-line biography is half a day of work (DS-04).
  2. Verify the current URL-prefix property first — the sitemap and robots file are already shipped (DS-07).

Blocks: DS-04, DS-07, site-reputation posture · Owner: Site owner

DI-07

Which front-page and imagery path should the next design iteration take?

Ready to decide — options drafted

What we have

  • Path A (shop-first front page, seasonal cards, original art) is shipped in this build.
  • The suggestion board holds the full option sets with effort and risk (DS-01, DS-02, DS-05).

What is missing

  • The owner’s pick between the drafted paths.
  • For the photography path: a unit in hand (DI-03).

Suggested ways to discover the missing information

  1. Read DS-01, DS-02 and DS-05 on the Suggestions board and mark one path per card.
  2. If unsure, keep Path A and revisit when measurement exists — the cost of waiting is zero.

Blocks: The next design iteration · Owner: Site owner

DI-08

What is the publishing cadence, and what topic goes next?

Open — partially decided

What we have

  • A fortnightly-guide cadence is recommended and drafted (SUG-06); four editorial hypotheses are already published as guides.
  • The build regenerates the sitemap and feed from the guide list, so every new guide is indexed automatically.

What is missing

  • Measured query and click data to choose topics from evidence rather than instinct.
  • A decision on whether the gifting and hosting guides continue past the season.

Suggested ways to discover the missing information

  1. Start the cadence now from the editorial hypothesis list and record each choice in the decisions log (DS-09).
  2. After 30 days of measurement, rank guides by read-through and outbound clicks, then pick the next topic (DI-04).

Blocks: The editorial calendar · Owner: Desk with the owner
