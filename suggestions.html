EDITORIAL DESK · OPTIONS, NOT ORDERS

Decide what to learn
before what to publish.

This is a reviewable proposal list. Topics below are editorial hypotheses, not keyword research or evidence of popularity. Choose work only after the unanswered questions are resolved.

Decision questions and discovery plan

Product identity · blocker

What exactly are the five products?

Need: intended ASIN, brand/model, official listing URL, variation, and source/confirmation date. Discover via enrolled Associates account/listing workflow; keep submitted ID and observed redirect as separate fields.

Audience · high value

Who is the buyer and what decision are they trying to make?

Need: audience, geography, budget context, use cases and accessibility needs. Discover through brief interviews, customer questions, and Search Console query data after indexing—not assumed personas.

Evidence · blocker

Which comparative claims can we support?

Need: dated official specifications, current listing details, methodology for hands-on tests, and permission/access where required. Until then, publish questions and explain gaps, not superiority claims.

Traffic · high value

Which topics have real reader demand?

Need: Search Console query/impression/click data, landing pages, date ranges and country/device dimensions. Set up property verification and sitemap submission; wait for data before calling a topic popular.

Measurement · blocker

What counts as a useful click or conversion?

Need: the five event names/definitions, privacy and retention plan, endpoint ownership, attribution limits, and official reports. No conversion event can be inferred from an outbound click.

Operations

Who reviews content and links, and how often?

Need: named reviewer, link test cadence, correction path, disclosure review, and product-data freshness standard. Recommend review before publication and on a dated recurring schedule.

Suggested editorial hypotheses

  1. Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?
  2. A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity
  3. How to assess cleanup and counter-space trade-offs
  4. Questions to ask before buying a multi-function kitchen appliance

These are working titles only. Product category and relevance to the supplied products remain unverified. First step: verify product identities, then choose the applicable questions.

Cadence proposal

One carefully sourced guide every two weeks for an initial 8-week test; review search and first-party campaign data monthly. This is a proposed operating cadence, not a claim about ranking outcomes. Pause if evidence, compliance review, or link validation is missing.
