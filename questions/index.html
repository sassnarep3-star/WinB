The pattern in the open questions

Most open items need something only the owner has: dashboard access, a decision about identity, or a small budget. That is not a research failure — it is the boundary of what a session without account access can settle. Where the answer is a check anyone with the login could do in ten minutes (Q-01, Q-11, Q-12), the row gives the steps and the time it takes.

Context note: rows in the missing table describe absences. There is no official source for information that does not exist yet, so those rows carry no source link and are marked unverified instead. When a question is answered, its row moves to Part one with the source attached — it is never answered from inference.
