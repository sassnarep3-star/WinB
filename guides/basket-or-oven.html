Basket or oven-style air fryer: what should you compare?

Choose a format by matching it to the way you cook—not by assuming one style is universally better. Check the current manufacturer specifications and retailer listing before you buy.

By Counterwise editorial · Updated 20 Nov 2026 · This is a general decision guide, not a product test.

Format changes how you load, access, and store an appliance. Compare usable cooking area and the shape of your usual food, opening clearance, countertop footprint, storage, and whether you can inspect or turn food conveniently. These are comparison prompts, not claims about any specific model.

Questions worth answering

  • What foods and portion sizes do you cook most often?
  • How much flat counter area and overhead clearance can you spare?
  • Would shelves or a basket suit your routine better? Verify the exact model’s configuration.
  • Where will removable parts be stored and washed?
  • Which cooking modes do you realistically expect to use?

Next step

Measure the intended spot, then compare the dimensions and included components on the official manufacturer page for each exact model.
