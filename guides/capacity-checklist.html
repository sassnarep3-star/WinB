THE BUYER’S NOTEBOOK · KITCHEN

A no-hype checklist for comparing air fryer capacity

Capacity labels alone may not answer whether a particular appliance fits your meals. Compare the usable area and the specific portions you prepare.

A capacity number is only useful when you understand what the manufacturer means by it. Look for exact dimensions, stated capacity units, cooking-area layout, and any maker guidance on batch size. Do not treat a larger headline number as proof of faster or better cooking.

Questions worth answering

  • How many people are you usually cooking for?
  • What is the shape and size of your most common batch?
  • Can the food fit in one layer if your recipe needs that? Check model instructions.
  • Would multiple batches be acceptable on busy evenings?
  • Will the appliance fit the available counter or cabinet space?

Next step

Compare official specifications and model instructions. If a specification or usable-area figure is not published, leave it unknown rather than estimating.
