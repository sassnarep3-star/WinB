Measurement status

Outbound clicks are counted locally in this browser using localStorage, keyed by the submitted identifier. They are not sent to a server, do not identify a person, and are not a site-wide analytics dataset. No analytics endpoint, cookie banner, campaign ledger entries, order reporting, or conversion attribution is active.

Before connecting analytics, decide on data minimization, consent and retention requirements, endpoint hosting, event schema, access controls, and applicable privacy requirements. The requested five events have not been defined in the materials available to this repository; do not invent them. Campaign ledger starts empty: timestamp, source, channel, eligible sessions, clicks, reported orders, dated source/provenance.

Editorial and compliance guardrails

  • Keep the Associates disclosure visible under the masthead and repeated in the footer.
  • Mark every outbound product link sponsored; do not describe supplied short links as invalid. Their precise status remains unresolved.
  • Review the current Associates Operating Agreement and FTC guidance before publication. This project does not certify compliance or provide legal advice.
  • Use current, authorized sources for product facts; avoid unsupported rankings, popularity, conversion or revenue claims.

Official review sources: Amazon Associates Program Policies / Operating Agreement; FTC: Endorsement Guides, what people are asking; Amazon Associates participation requirements.
