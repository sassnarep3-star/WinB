Why the two ASIN fields are separate

A short link is an opaque code. What it resolves to today is an observation; what you meant it to point at is an intention. If they are stored in one field, a change at the shortener silently rewrites your own record. Amazon’s ASIN is a stable, checkable identifier — so it belongs in its own column, confirmed and dated.

What the tag column shows

None of the five observed redirects contains a tag= parameter. That is recorded as observed, not interpreted. The site publishes tagged links so that clicks are attributable regardless of how the short-link question resolves. FLG-001.
