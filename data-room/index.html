A2

Why the two ASIN fields are separate

A short link is an opaque code. What it resolves to today is an observation; what you meant it to point at is an intention. If they are stored in one field, a change at the shortener silently rewrites your own record. Amazon’s ASIN is a stable, checkable identifier — so it belongs in its own column, confirmed and dated.

What the tag column shows

None of the five observed redirects contains a tag= parameter. That is recorded as observed, not interpreted. The site publishes tagged links so that clicks are attributable regardless of how the short-link question resolves. FLG-001.

U2 Food Thermometers (factsheet, incl. Calibration: ice-water and boiling-water methods)
USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service		 — 2026-11-20 • Ice-water calibration: fill a large glass with ice cubes, add drinking water, stir well; immerse the stem at least 2 inches without touching the sides or bottom; wait at least 30 seconds and read without removing the stem. It should read 32°F.
• Boiling-water calibration: the thermometer should read 212°F at sea level; at high altitude water boils lower — by at least 2°F and perhaps as much as 5°F; check the local Cooperative Extension System office or health department for the exact figure.
• If a thermometer does not allow manual calibration, it may need to be replaced.
• Most food thermometers give an accurate reading within 2 to 4°F; many dial thermometers have a calibration nut under the dial.
• Wireless and smart thermometers: the probe stays in the food while cooking and displays on a connected device; smart thermometers can be calibrated.
• Thermometer care: wash with hot, soapy water; most thermometers should not be immersed in water.
