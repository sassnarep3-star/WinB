Category · Air fryers

Air fryers: three shapes, one decision.

Dual basket, oven style, or one big drawer. Before capacity numbers and preset counts, the shape decides what your weeknights look like.

What to compare in this category

  • Shape first: basket drawers shine for one big crispy batch; oven styles take racks, trays and toast duty; dual baskets sync two foods.
  • Footprint honesty: measure the counter and the space above — doors and drawers need clearance too.
  • Capacity is a shape question: quarts in a tall basket are not quarts across a wide rack. Run the checklist.
  • Cleanup is the recurring cost: every part that touches food should be dishwasherable — or you'll resent it by week three.
  • Recalls: the CPSC database has an Air Fryer category; check it before you buy (cpsc.gov/Recalls).
