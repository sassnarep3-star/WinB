The working record, in order. It includes the things that failed — a blocked host, a disabled workflow, a page that would not give up a reliable number — because a log that only records successes is a marketing document.

Each link was opened once and the redirect target recorded. Results: B04G1MEwY to B0CDY277D3, B02NbDxp3 to B09B7SB46R, B00hJ57DV to B08DKYBTPH, B0c8moW59 to B0DHZTCNR5, B0f3GKalj to B0DZFD6R9Z. Sections inspected: Commission Income Statement, Participation Requirements, Trademark Guidelines, IP License and Usage Requirements, and the Agent Terms inside the IP License.		Three clauses changed the build: price display is restricted to Amazon-served or API data, images may not be cached, and agents must identify themselves in the user agent string.S02
R-0705:47Read the Associates Program Operating Agreement
Section 5 was read in full for the exact disclosure sentence, and Section 6 for the termination terms.		The required sentence was copied verbatim and is now injected into every page twice, once under the masthead and once in the footer.S01
R-0805:50Read the FTC disclosures guidance for endorsers
The page was retrieved in full.		Two rules are now enforced editorially: a disclosure placed only at the end of a page is likely to be missed, and no claim of experience may be made for a product the publisher has not tried. The second rule is why the site never says tested.S05
R-0905:53Attempted to read the Seller Central ASIN glossary

R-1005:56Read Google's sitemap documentation
Checked which sitemap formats Google accepts and whether it states a preference.		Google states no preference among supported formats. The site therefore ships an XML sitemap for crawlers and an RSS feed for readers, both generated from the same data.S07
R-1106:00Tried to attribute a price and a star rating to each product
The retrieved page text interleaves the viewed product with 'customers who viewed this item also viewed' entries, and prices in both blocks render identically.		Attribution could not be made with confidence, and the policy clause independently forbids static price display. Both fields are left blank on the site and explained on the product table page. Recorded as FLG-004.S02
