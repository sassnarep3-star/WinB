Source register

A source appears here only if it was actually fetched during the build. Every row records the publisher, the date on the document, the retrieval date, and what the document is cited for. Sources that could not be reached are listed too, and marked.

Evidence tier — what "verified" is allowed to mean here

The sources below were not all checked to the same depth, and the register says so rather than implying a single standard. A — full text: the document was retrieved and the relevant section read end to end. B — section: the specific section cited was retrieved and quoted, but the whole document was not. C — secondary: a third party, used for orientation only and never to support a claim.
