What this machine decides

A 26-quart French-door oven with 24 presets and six accessories included, for cooks who want one box to do more than fry.

A 26-quart French-door oven is a different category of object from a basket fryer, and the reviews that compare them directly tend to miss that. It is a countertop convection oven with a door, which means flat things fit — a tray of vegetables for four, a pizza, a sheet of toast — and it means you need front clearance for the door to swing. Twenty-four presets suggest the manufacturer expects you to lean on them; whether that is convenience or clutter depends on whether you keep the card. The six included accessories are the part worth checking against what you already own, because accessories you do not use are just storage.

What to compare it on

  1. Counter depth: an oven this size needs front clearance for the door swing, not just wall space.
  2. Whether you want presets or dial control — 24 presets help only if you keep the cheat sheet.
  3. Which of the six accessories you would genuinely use in the first month.
