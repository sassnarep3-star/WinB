What this machine decides

One 8-quart basket with a 450°F top range — the largest single-basket capacity in this set.

This is the straightforward answer to 'I want one big basket.' Eight quarts in a single drawer with a 450°F top end, which is the highest ceiling in this set. That top range matters for crisping and for finishing, and it is also the number most people never touch. The real question is shape: an 8-quart single basket holds a lot, but a deep basket is worse than a shallow one for anything you want spread in a single layer. If most of your cooking is one tray of something for a family, this is the least complicated machine here. If it is two different things, it is the wrong shape entirely.

What to compare it on

  1. Basket depth versus width: an 8-qt basket holds a lot, but tall items still need headroom.
  2. Whether 450°F is a setting you will use or a number that decides the purchase.
  3. Hand-wash effort on the basket rim, which is where coatings usually fail first.
