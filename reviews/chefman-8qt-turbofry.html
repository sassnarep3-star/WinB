The arithmetic, shown

8 qt in one basket is 2 qt more than the 6 qt dual-basket total, held in a single cavity.

8 − 6 = 2

Compares stated totals only. The dual-basket unit cannot fry one 8 qt batch, and this unit cannot fry two foods separately.
