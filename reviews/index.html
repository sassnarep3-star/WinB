The shortlist

Five products, and what each one is for

Not a ranked list. Three air fryers and two thermometers that solve different problems, each with the same page structure: who it suits, what the listing claims, and what nobody can tell you yet.

There is no “best” here, and that is deliberate

A dual-basket drawer, a big single basket and a French-door oven are not competitors. Naming one the winner would be a ranking of three different jobs. Read the “when to skip it” line on each page — that is the part that decides it.
